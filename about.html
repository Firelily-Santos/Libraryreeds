A little about the storyteller.

I'm Firelily Santos, the author behind Silverthorne Academy and the creator of Libraryreeds.

My stories explore magical worlds, unexpected powers, and the courage it takes to step into the unknown. Libraryreeds is a place to discover my writing and share in the worlds taking shape on the page.

Thank you for being here. Whether you're discovering a story for the first time or returning to see what's next, I'm glad you're part of the journey.
